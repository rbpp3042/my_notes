bb-agent — Privacy policy

bb-agent is a private tool used only by its owner, Yegor Kolosov. It is not available to other users.

The app accesses Google Calendar and Gmail data only for accounts that its owner controls, and only to carry out tasks the owner requests. Data is processed on the owner's own servers and is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
